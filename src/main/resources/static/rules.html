League Rules & Information

Please read the following rules and costing guidelines carefully before the season begins.

Entry Fee & Costing

Cost: $100 per player for the entire season.

All money must be paid in full via Venmo prior to the start of the season. No exceptions.

How To Play & Pick Rules

  • Pick Every Game: You must select a winning team for every game scheduled during the week. Picks are made against the spread.
  • The Double Pick: Every week, you must designate exactly one game as your Double Pick. If your Double Pick is correct, you earn 2 points instead of the standard 1 point. If it's wrong, you get 0.
  • Lock Times & Kickoff: All picks must be submitted and saved prior to kickoff of each individual game. You can modify any unscheduled/unstarted game picks right up until their official start times.
  • Strict Missed Picks Policy: If you miss a kickoff, you miss the pick. Missed picks are counted as a Loss (0 points). There will be NO commissioner overrides or late additions under any circumstances. If you miss, you miss.

Weekly Winners & Ties

  • The player who accumulates the highest total points in a given week (including the Double Pick extra point) is declared the Weekly Winner.
  • Tie Splitting: If multiple players tie for the highest score of the week, the weekly cash prize is split equally among all tied players.

Season Standings & Prizes

At the end of the 17-week season, the top three players with the highest overall cumulative points will be paid out from the remaining season-end cash pool.

Standing Place Prize Percentage
1st Place 60% of the Season Pool
2nd Place 30% of the Season Pool
3rd Place 10% of the Season Pool

Note: Ties in the final season standings will have their prize money combined and split equally (e.g. if two players tie for 1st, they split the 1st & 2nd place cash pools, receiving 45% each).

Dynamic Prize Pool Formula

To guarantee a solid season-end prize pool for the top 3 players, the weekly winner payout scales dynamically based on the total number of players (N) who join:

  • At least 20 players (N ≥ 20): The weekly prize is a full $100 per week (totaling $1,700 for the 17 weeks). The remaining leftover cash becomes the Season-End Pool.
  • Fewer than 20 players (N < 20): The weekly prize is scaled down to $5 × N per week. This guarantees that exactly 15% of all entry fees ($15 × N) is preserved to pay out the top 3 overall finishers.
Live Math Examples:
  • With 25 players: Total pool is $2,500. Weekly payout is $100. Leftover Season-End pool is $800 (1st: $480, 2nd: $240, 3rd: $80).
  • With 18 players: Total pool is $1,800. Weekly payout is $90. Leftover Season-End pool is $270 (1st: $162, 2nd: $81, 3rd: $27).
  • With 15 players: Total pool is $1,500. Weekly payout is $75. Leftover Season-End pool is $225 (1st: $135, 2nd: $67.50, 3rd: $22.50).
